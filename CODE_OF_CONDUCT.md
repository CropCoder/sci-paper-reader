# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent; critique ideas, not people.
- Give and accept actionable feedback.
- Respect privacy and do not share unpublished papers, personal data, or credentials.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sharing copyrighted PDFs or generated text in violation of the source license.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block contributors who violate this code. To report
an incident, open a private security advisory or contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile.

This code is adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
